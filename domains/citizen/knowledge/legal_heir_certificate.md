## Steps
1. Get the death certificate first.
2. Apply at an e-Sevai centre or online: list every legal heir (spouse, children, and parents where applicable) with Aadhaar, the deceased's address proof and the applicant's ID.
3. The VAO and Revenue Inspector enquire with neighbours and family; the Tahsildar issues the certificate.
4. Use it for patta transfer, bank and post office claims, insurance, and family pension.

## Tips
- Name every heir, including married daughters; leaving someone out leads to disputes and rejection later.
- For large estates or disputes, banks and courts may ask for a succession certificate from a civil court instead.

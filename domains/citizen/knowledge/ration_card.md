## Steps
- New card: TNPDS portal → apply for a new smart card; upload Aadhaar of all members, address proof, a family photo. A new card usually needs proof that the family is separate from the parents' card.
- Changes (add a newborn or a spouse, remove a member, change address or shop): through the same portal or e-Sevai centre.
- Status: track with the reference number; you'll get an SMS when it's approved.

## Using the card
- Fingerprint or OTP at the ration shop; one-nation-one-card lets migrant workers draw rations in other states.
- Complaints about shortages or denial: the district supply office or the state grievance helpline.

**Process:** e-Sevai centre / TNeGA e-District portal → online application with scanned documents → VAO report → Revenue Inspector → Tahsildar approval → digitally signed certificate (downloadable; verifiable by QR code).

**Documents:** Aadhaar, ration card or address proof, passport photo, income proof (salary certificate, or self-declaration of farm/daily-wage income), and any land documents if farming income.

**Validity:** often treated as valid for the financial year — check what the scheme or institution requires.

**If delayed:** check status with the application number on the portal; if it crosses the service time limit, petition the Tahsildar or use the CM Helpline (1100).

**Grievance:** state the problem, location, since when, who you already contacted (with dates/receipt numbers), and the specific action you want. One issue per petition.

**Escalation:** if no action within the stated time, escalate on the same portal or petition the District Collector (weekly grievance day meetings in TN are usually on Mondays).

**RTI (Right to Information Act, 2005):** write to the Public Information Officer (PIO) of the office. Ask specific questions, e.g. "Status of application no. X dated Y, with names of officers it is pending with and reasons for delay." Fee ₹10 (BPL exempt). Reply due in 30 days; first appeal to the First Appellate Authority if no reply; then the State Information Commission.

**Bribery:** report to the Directorate of Vigilance and Anti-Corruption (DVAC) in Tamil Nadu.

## Steps
1. Apply through an e-Sevai centre or the e-District portal.
2. Documents: parent's or sibling's community certificate (strongly helps), school transfer certificate showing community, Aadhaar, ration card, photo.
3. VAO and Revenue Inspector enquiry; the competent authority (usually the Tahsildar; for some communities a higher revenue officer) approves.
4. Download the digitally signed certificate; it's verifiable by QR code.

## Tips
- Apply well before admission or job deadlines; enquiries can take weeks.
- A rejected application gets a written reason; you can appeal to the higher revenue officer.

**Central schemes (NSAP):** Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension (60+, BPL), Widow Pension (40+, BPL), Disability Pension (18+, severe disability, BPL). States add their own top-up and run additional schemes.

**Tamil Nadu:** the Social Security Scheme (Revenue Dept) covers old-age, widows, destitute / deserted women, differently-abled, unmarried women above a certain age, and others. Conditions typically include low income, no substantial property and no earning family support — check current rules.

**Documents:** Aadhaar, age proof, ration card, bank passbook, photo, and specific proof (death certificate of husband, disability certificate, etc.).

**Pension stopped?** Usually due to Aadhaar/bank mismatch, a missed life certificate, or a verification flag. Ask the Social Security Tahsildar for the reason in writing.

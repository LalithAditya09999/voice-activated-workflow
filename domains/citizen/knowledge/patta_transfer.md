## Steps
1. Apply online on the Tamil Nadu e-Services land portal or at an e-Sevai centre ("patta transfer").
2. Upload or carry: registered sale deed (or gift / settlement deed), encumbrance certificate, previous patta, Aadhaar. For inheritance: death certificate and legal heir certificate.
3. The VAO and revenue officials verify; if the land must be split (sub-division), a surveyor measures it.
4. The new patta can be downloaded online; check the name, survey number and extent carefully.

## Tips
- In Tamil Nadu, registrations often trigger the transfer automatically for whole-survey-number purchases; part purchases need sub-division. Check the status online with your application number.
- Many schemes (PM-KISAN, crop insurance, drip subsidy) need the land in your name, so do this first.
- Never pay anyone beyond the official fee; ask for the receipt.

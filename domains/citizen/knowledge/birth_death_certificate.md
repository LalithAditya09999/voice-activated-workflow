## Steps
- Hospital births and deaths are usually reported by the hospital; check with the registrar that it's done.
- Home events: report to the village panchayat or municipal registrar within 21 days.
- Late registration: after 21 days, after 30 days and after a year need progressively more approvals (and an affidavit or magistrate's order for over a year).
- Child's name can be added to a birth registration later, within the time the rules allow; do it before school admission.
- Certificates: download from the Civil Registration System portal or collect from the registrar.

## Why it matters
- A death certificate is needed for pension stoppage or family pension, insurance, bank accounts and patta transfer (with a legal heir certificate).

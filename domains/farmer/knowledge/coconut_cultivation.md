## Conventional practice
- Spacing about 7.5 m × 7.5 m (around 70 palms per acre). Drip: 4 drippers per palm.
- Fertiliser per adult palm per year, in two splits (June–July and Dec–Jan), as per the TNAU package: urea, super phosphate and muriate of potash, with 50 kg farmyard manure. Follow your soil-test card for exact amounts.
- Pest control: hook out rhinoceros beetle from the crown; pheromone traps for red palm weevil; plug holes and treat only as recommended.

## Organic / natural practice
- Husk burial in trenches between rows to hold water; coir-pith compost; green manure (sunhemp, daincha) in the basin.
- Intercrops: banana, cocoa, pepper or vegetables in the gaps add income and shade the soil.
- Treat breeding sites of rhinoceros beetle (manure pits) with the green muscardine fungus (Metarhizium) from the agriculture depot.

## Side effects & risks
- Excess potash without organic matter does not stop button shedding; water stress in summer does. Mulch and drip first.
- Indiscriminate root-feeding of insecticide leaves residues in tender nuts; use only recommended products and waiting periods.

**Help:** Coconut Development Board schemes (replanting, new planting) through the horticulture office.

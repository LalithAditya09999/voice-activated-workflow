**Taking a sample:** after harvest, before ploughing. From 8–10 spots in a zig-zag, dig a V-shaped pit 15 cm deep (paddy, vegetables) or deeper for tree crops, take a slice, mix, reduce to ~500 g, air-dry in shade, label with name and survey number.

**Reading the card:** N, P, K, pH, EC, organic carbon and micronutrients (zinc, iron, boron…). Follow the crop-wise dose on the card; split nitrogen into 3–4 doses.

**Saving money:** excess urea makes plants soft and invites pests (leaf folder, BPH in paddy). Use neem-coated urea. Zinc sulphate for zinc-deficient paddy soils is common in TN. Combine with green manure and compost to cut chemical fertiliser.

**Buying:** subsidised fertilisers are sold through Aadhaar-authenticated PoS machines. Insist on a printed bill; report overcharging or forced "tagging" of other products to the agriculture officer.

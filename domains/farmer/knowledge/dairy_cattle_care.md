## Practice
- Fodder: grow Co-4/Co-5 grass, fodder sorghum, agathi, subabul or desmanthus on a small plot; chop before feeding.
- Concentrate by milk yield; mineral mixture daily (prevents repeat breeding and low milk).
- Health: foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) vaccine every 6 months (free campaigns through the veterinary dispensary), deworming, tick control.
- Clean milking: wash and dry the udder, full-hand milking, dip teats after milking; check for mastitis early (lumps, watery milk).

## Native and crossbred
- Crossbreds give more milk but need more feed and care; native breeds are hardy and cheaper to keep (see "Native livestock breeds").

## Selling and support
- Sell to the village milk cooperative (Aavin in Tamil Nadu) for a steady price; ask about cattle insurance and breeding services at the veterinary dispensary.

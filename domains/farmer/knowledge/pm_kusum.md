**Component B — standalone off-grid solar pumps** (up to 7.5 HP; larger allowed in some cases): central financial assistance ~30% of benchmark cost (higher in some hilly/NE states), state ~30%, farmer ~40% (bank loan may cover up to ~30%). Tamil Nadu has at times given a higher state share — check the current scheme.

**Component C — solarising existing grid pumps:** panels on your existing pump; extra power can be sold to the DISCOM.

**Good for:** farms without an EB connection, or with unreliable night-time power. Pump size must match well yield and depth — ask for a yield test.

**Apply:** Agricultural Engineering Department (district Executive Engineer office / online portal in TN). Beware fake "KUSUM registration" websites asking for fees — only use the official state/MNRE portal.

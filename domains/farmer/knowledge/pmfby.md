**Premium paid by farmer:** 2% (kharif), 1.5% (rabi), 5% (annual commercial / horticulture). Government pays the rest.

**Enrolment:** voluntary for all farmers (including loanee farmers, who can opt out). Enrol before the season's cut-off date through the bank, CSC/e-Sevai, the PMFBY portal or the Crop Insurance app. Tamil Nadu notifies crops and cut-off dates each season (e.g. separate dates for Kuruvai and Samba paddy).

**Covered:** prevented sowing, standing crop loss (drought, flood, pests, disease, etc. — assessed at area level), localised calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, natural fire) assessed per farm, post-harvest loss from cyclonic rain / unseasonal rain for crops drying in the field up to 14 days.

**Claim for localised / post-harvest loss:** inform within **72 hours** — call the insurance company's toll-free number, use the Crop Insurance app, or tell the bank / agriculture officer. Give: name, survey number, crop, cause, date, area affected. Keep dated photos. A surveyor assesses within days. Settlement goes to the bank account.

**Helpline:** Krishi Rakshak portal & helpline 14447.

## Conventional practice
- Bower (pandal) system; pruning decides the harvest timing, so plan prunings to avoid the heaviest rain.
- Fertigation through drip by growth stage; gibberellic acid and other regulators only as recommended.
- Downy mildew (wet, humid weather) and powdery mildew (dry weather): monitor leaves, spray recommended fungicides with the correct waiting period before harvest.

## Organic / natural practice
- Compost and green manure under the vines; mulching; panchagavya and fish amino acid sprays at shoot growth.
- Open canopy (shoot thinning) and good air flow reduce mildew; Trichoderma/Pseudomonas in soil.

## Side effects & risks
- Residues above limits block sales and exports: keep a spray diary and respect waiting periods.
- Heavy rain at harvest cracks berries: time pruning and harvest with the forecast.

**Know your product:** Cumbum Panneer grapes are reported to have received a GI (geographical indication) tag (confirm), which helps sell at a premium as a named product.

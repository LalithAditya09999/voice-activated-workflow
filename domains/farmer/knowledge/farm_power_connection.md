**Process:** apply at the section office of the distribution company (TNPDCL, formerly TANGEDCO) or online → registration number → seniority list by date and category → field inspection → estimate → connection.

**Documents:** patta/chitta/adangal, FMB sketch with the well marked, VAO certificate of well ownership, Aadhaar, and consent from co-owners if the land is jointly held.

**Check before applying:** whether your block is notified as over-exploited for groundwater — new borewells there may need permission, which affects new connections.

**Alternatives while you wait:** PM-KUSUM solar pump (no EB connection needed), or solarising an existing pump.

**Watch out:** officials can't legally charge anything beyond the published fee/estimate. Ask for a receipt for every payment.

**Buying checklist:** buy from a known breeder, a government livestock farm, or a recognised shandy; check age by teeth, bright eyes, moist muzzle, normal dung, no lameness; ask for vaccination record (FMD, HS, PPR for goats/sheep); insist on ear-tag registration (INAPH / Pashu Aadhaar); quarantine new animals 2–3 weeks; insure through the livestock insurance scheme.

**Dealer directory:** verified breeders/dealers for native and other breeds will be listed here after verification (name, village, breeds, phone, verified-by). Never pay advance to unknown online sellers.

**Support:** veterinary dispensaries and TANUVAS give advice; ask about breed conservation and livestock insurance schemes.

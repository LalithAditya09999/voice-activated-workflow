**Benefit:** ₹6,000 per year per landholding farmer family, paid as three instalments of ₹2,000 by DBT.

**Excluded:** institutional landholders; families where a member is a constitutional post holder, current/former minister/MP/MLA, serving or retired government employee (except Group D / MTS), pensioners with ₹10,000+ monthly pension, income-tax payers, and practising professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs, architects).

**Register:** pmkisan.gov.in → "New Farmer Registration", or at a CSC / e-Sevai centre. Land details are verified by the state revenue department.

**Instalment stopped? Check in this order:** (1) e-KYC done? (OTP on pmkisan.gov.in or biometric at CSC; face authentication via app) (2) bank account Aadhaar-seeded and NPCI-mapped? (3) land seeding status "Yes"? (4) name mismatch between Aadhaar and records? Use "Know Your Status" on the portal. Helpline: 155261 / 011-24300606.

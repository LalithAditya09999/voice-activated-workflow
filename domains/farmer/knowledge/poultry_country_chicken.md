| | Country chicken (natural / backyard) | Commercial broiler / layer |
|---|---|---|
| Feed | Forages insects, weeds, grain spill; little supplement (broken grain, azolla, termites) | 100% bought feed; feed is ~70% of cost |
| Growth / eggs | Slow (several months to sale); 50–100 eggs/yr | Broiler ~2 kg in ~6 weeks; layers 250–300+ eggs/yr |
| Price | Premium, sold locally | Low margin per bird; volume business, often contract farming |
| Health | Hardy; still needs vaccination | High disease risk in dense sheds; biosecurity essential |
| Side effects / risks | Predators (cover at night); slower cash flow | Misuse of antibiotics as growth promoters drives antimicrobial resistance (banned for growth promotion in India); manure/odour; market price swings |

**Natural-farming integration:** chickens in orchards and after harvest eat pests and weed seeds and add manure; poultry litter compost feeds crops. Improved dual-purpose birds from TANUVAS/government hatcheries give more eggs while still foraging.

**Vaccination (ask the vet for your area's schedule):** Ranikhet/Newcastle disease (F/Lasota early, R2B booster), fowl pox; deworm regularly.

## Practice
- Sow with the first assured rains (Aadi pattam, July–Aug; Purattasi pattam, Sep–Oct). Line sowing makes weeding easier.
- Intercrops: cumbu or cholam with cowpea, redgram or groundnut.
- Nutrition: farmyard manure plus a small soil-test-based dose (conventional), or compost and jeevamirtham (organic). Millets respond to little.
- Harvest when grains are hard; dry well before storing.

## Market
- Growing demand for millet foods; FPOs and self-help groups add value (flour, flakes). Ask about state millet mission support for seed, processing machines and marketing.

## Side effects & risks
- Bird damage on samai, thinai and varagu near harvest: harvest on time, use reflective tapes.
- Processing (dehulling) is the bottleneck for small millets; plan with a group or FPO.

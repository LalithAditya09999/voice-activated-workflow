## Conventional practice
- Pro-tray nursery under insect net; hybrids or improved varieties suited to the season.
- Drip with fertigation by stage; staking or trellis for tomato.
- Recommended insecticides only after the threshold, rotating groups; respect the waiting period before picking.

## Organic / natural practice
- Border crop of maize or sorghum, marigold trap crop for tomato fruit borer.
- Pheromone traps (brinjal shoot and fruit borer, tomato fruit borer), yellow sticky traps (whitefly), NSKE 5%, neem oil.
- Remove and destroy infested shoots and fruits weekly.

## Side effects & risks
- Vegetables are eaten fresh: residues matter most here. Never spray just before picking.
- Repeating tomato or brinjal in the same field carries over wilt and nematodes: rotate.

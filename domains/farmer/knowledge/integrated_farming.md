**A 2-acre example (adapt to your land and water):**
- 1.2 acre field crops in rotation (paddy/millet → pulses → green manure)
- 0.3 acre vegetables and fodder (Co-4/Co-5 grass, agathi, subabul, desmanthus)
- 2 native or crossbred cows + 10 goats; biogas from dung; slurry to fields
- 20–30 country chickens; azolla pit as protein feed
- Farm pond (fish: catla, rohu, mrigal) with fodder/fruit trees on bunds
- Living fence: kiluvai, glyricidia, agathi — fodder + green leaf manure + protection
- Five-layer orchard on bunds/boundary (Nammalvar's ஐந்தடுக்கு): coconut / mango → banana/papaya → guava/lemon → vegetables → tubers/greens

**Native vs crossbred cattle — side by side:** crossbred (Jersey/HF crosses) give much more milk but need more feed, cooling and vet care; native breeds give less milk but are hardy, cheap to keep, valued for dung/urine-based inputs and draught. Choose by your fodder, water and market.

**Support to ask about:** farm pond and IFS schemes under the agriculture/horticulture departments, MGNREGA works for farm ponds/bunds, livestock insurance, NLM schemes for goat/poultry units.

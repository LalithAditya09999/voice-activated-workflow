## Options
| Where | For | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Uzhavar Sandhai (farmers' markets) | vegetables, fruits | no commission; get a farmer ID card from the market officer |
| Regulated markets | grains, pulses, cotton, copra | transparent weighing and auction; storage and pledge loans |
| Direct Purchase Centres (TNCSC) | paddy | support price; register in advance, bring land details |
| eNAM | many crops | online prices and bids across markets |
| FPOs | everything | group selling, grading, contracts, bulk inputs |

## Tips
- Check the day's price before loading; grade and clean produce; keep the bill or receipt.
- Don't sell at harvest if prices are at their lowest: see "Grain storage and pledge loans".

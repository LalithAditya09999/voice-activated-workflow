## How (both conventional and organic growers use it)
- Seed: about 2–3 kg per acre (instead of 20–30 kg). Mat nursery; transplant 14-day-old seedlings, one per hill.
- Spacing 25 cm × 25 cm in a square, so the weeder can run both ways.
- Water: irrigate to about 2.5 cm after the water disappears and hairline cracks appear, until flowering; then keep a thin layer.
- Weeding: rotary (cono) weeder at 10–15 day intervals, which also aerates the soil.
- Nutrition: compost or green manure plus soil-test-based fertiliser (conventional), or jeevamirtham and green manure (organic).

## Side effects & risks
- Needs careful levelling and timely weeding; the first season takes more labour.
- Not suited where you cannot control water (deep, flooded fields).

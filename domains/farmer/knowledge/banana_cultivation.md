## Conventional practice
- Spacing about 1.8 m × 1.8 m; tissue-culture plants (e.g. Grand Naine) give uniform bunches.
- Fertigation: split N and K into weekly doses through drip; follow the soil test.
- Desucker regularly, prop with bamboo or ropes at bunching, cover bunches to protect from sunscald and pests.

## Organic / natural practice
- Farmyard manure or vermicompost in the pit, Azospirillum and phosphobacteria, mulching with trash.
- Trap pseudostem weevil with split-stem traps; use Pseudomonas / Trichoderma for wilt-prone soils.

## Side effects & risks
- Panama wilt (Fusarium) stays in the soil for years: rotate with paddy or other crops; varieties like Rasthali are susceptible.
- Bunchy top spreads through aphids and infected suckers: never take suckers from a sick field.

**Insurance:** banana is a horticulture crop under PMFBY (farmer premium up to 5%). **Selling:** Theni and nearby markets, FPOs, and direct buyers for tissue-culture bunches.

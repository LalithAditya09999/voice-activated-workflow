| Variety | Known for | Season fit (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Mappillai Samba | Red rice, hardy, traditionally valued for strength | Samba (long duration) |
| Karuppu Kavuni | Black rice from the Chettinad region, premium price | Samba |
| Seeraga Samba | Tiny aromatic grain, used for biryani, premium price | Samba |
| Kichili Samba | Fine white grain, good cooking quality | Samba |
| Thooyamalli | Fine white "jasmine-like" grain | Samba / Thaladi |
| Poongar | Short duration, traditionally given to women; tolerates waterlogging | Kuruvai / Navarai |
| Kattuyanam | Very tall, tolerant of flood and drought, low input | Samba (long) |
| Kullakar / Kuzhiyadichan | Hardy red rices for dry or saline patches | varies |

Durations vary by location — ask the seed-saver farmer about performance in your district.

**Where to get seed:** the annual Nel Thiruvizha (paddy seed festival) tradition started by the late Nel Jayaraman in Thiruvarur district, organic farmer associations, and farmer-to-farmer exchange (often "borrow 1 kg, return 2 kg"). Some state seed farms and TNAU also maintain traditional varieties.

**Selling:** traditional rice sells at a premium through organic stores, FPOs and direct-to-consumer; certification (PGS-India under PKVY) helps.

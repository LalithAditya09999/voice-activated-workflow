Proportions below are common versions; trainers differ. Always test a new spray on a few plants first.

**Amirtha karaisal (soil tonic)** — 10 kg fresh cow dung + 10 L cow urine + 1 kg jaggery (or ripe fruit) + 100–200 L water. Stir, keep covered in shade for 1–2 days. Pour along irrigation channels or near plant roots. Use fresh.

**Meen amilam / fish amino acid (growth promoter)** — fish waste and jaggery in equal weight, packed in an airtight container, kept in shade ~20–30 days until it smells like fruit. Filter. Spray 2–5 ml per litre of water on vegetative crops.

**Thema mor karaisal (coconut milk + buttermilk; flowering & fungal checks)** — 1 L coconut milk + 5 L sour buttermilk, ferment 7 days in a closed pot. Spray about 1 L in 10 L water.

**Mooligai poochi viratti (herbal pest repellent)** — 1 kg each of bitter/strong-smelling leaves that grow locally (neem, nochi, adathoda, erukku, pungam, custard apple) crushed and soaked in 10 L cow urine for 7–10 days. Filter; spray 1 L in 10 L water. Repels; doesn't kill instantly.

**Panchagavya, jeevamirtham, vermicompost, green manure** — see "Organic manures and bio-inputs".

**Safety:** wear gloves; keep fermenting drums away from children; use clean water; erukku (calotropis) sap irritates eyes and skin.

**Steps**
1. **Look weekly**: walk the field in a zig-zag; check 10 plants at different spots. Note pest, stage and % damage.
2. **Prevention**: resistant varieties, clean seed, timely sowing, balanced fertiliser (excess nitrogen invites leaf folder and brown planthopper), alleys every 2–3 m in paddy, bund crops.
3. **Traps**: pheromone traps (stem borer, fruit borers), yellow sticky traps (whitefly, aphids), light traps.
4. **Biological**: Trichogramma egg cards (stem borer, sugarcane borers), Pseudomonas fluorescens (blast, sheath blight), Trichoderma (root rots) — available at agriculture depots.
5. **Botanicals**: neem seed kernel extract (NSKE) 5% — soak 5 kg crushed kernels per 100 L water overnight, filter, add a little soap; neem oil 3% with emulsifier.
6. **Chemical (last resort)**: only after crossing the economic threshold level, only products recommended for that crop and pest, at the label dose. Never mix several pesticides. Wear a mask and gloves; never spray against the wind; keep away from children and water sources. Buy from licensed dealers and keep the bill.

**Never** use banned / restricted pesticides, and never use pesticide containers for water or food.

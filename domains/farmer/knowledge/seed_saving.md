**Selecting:** mark the best plants before harvest (uniform height and duration, no disease, well-filled grain). Remove off-types. Harvest separately.

**Drying & storing:** sun-dry until the grain cracks cleanly when bitten (paddy ~12% moisture). Store in clean mud pots, metal bins or hermetic bags; add dried neem / nochi leaves; keep off the floor; check monthly.

**Seed treatment before sowing (organic):** soak in diluted cow urine or treat with Pseudomonas / Trichoderma bio-agents from the agriculture office; beejamrutham is used in natural farming.

**Seed cycling / exchange:** grow a variety for 3–4 seasons, then exchange seed with another farmer to keep vigour; keep a register of who borrowed what. Village seed banks can be run by FPOs or women's SHGs.

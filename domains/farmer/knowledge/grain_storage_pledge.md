**Regulated markets (TN Agricultural Marketing Dept):** storage godowns at low rent; **pledge loan** against stored produce (a large share of its value, at concessional interest; ask for the current limit and interest-free period); transparent weighing and e-auction; payment to bank account.

**Warehouses:** Tamil Nadu Warehousing Corporation and Central Warehousing Corporation. WDRA-registered warehouses issue an electronic negotiable warehouse receipt (e-NWR), which banks accept for loans.

**Prices:** eNAM (online mandi trading), Agmarknet daily prices, TNAU market price forecasts before sowing/harvest.

**Selling direct:** Uzhavar Sandhai (farmers' markets) for vegetables and fruits — no commission; FPOs for collective selling and bulk input buying.

**Storing at home:** dry grain well (paddy ~12–13% moisture); clean, fumigation-free storage with neem leaves; hermetic bags; raised platforms; check for insects monthly.

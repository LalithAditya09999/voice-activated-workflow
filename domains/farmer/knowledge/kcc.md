**Interest:** 7% with 1.5% interest subvention to banks and 3% prompt repayment incentive to farmers → ~4% effective, on short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh (the Union Budget 2025-26 announced raising this limit to ₹5 lakh — confirm with your bank).

**Collateral:** not needed up to the RBI limit for collateral-free agriculture loans (raised to ₹2 lakh from January 2025).

**Also covers:** animal husbandry and fisheries working capital; RuPay card for withdrawals; crop insurance linked.

**Where:** commercial banks, regional rural banks, and Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies (PACCS) in Tamil Nadu.

**Loan waivers / debt relief:** Tamil Nadu has waived cooperative crop loans in the past (for example in 2021). Waivers are announced by government order with specific cut-off dates and eligibility — never pay anyone to "get your name in the waiver list". Check with your cooperative society or bank directly.

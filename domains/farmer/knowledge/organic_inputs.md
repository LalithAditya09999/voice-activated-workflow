**Jeevamirtham (per acre):** 200 L water + 10 kg fresh native-cow dung + 5–10 L cow urine + 2 kg jaggery + 2 kg pulse flour + a handful of soil from the bund. Ferment in shade 48–72 h, stir clockwise twice daily, use within ~7 days. Apply with irrigation water or as a 10% foliar spray (filtered).

**Panchagavya (TNAU method, approx.):** mix 7 kg fresh cow dung + 1 kg ghee, keep 3 days stirring daily; then add 10 L cow urine + 10 L water and stir for 15 days; then add 3 L milk, 2 L curd, 3 L tender coconut water, 3 kg jaggery and well-ripened bananas. Ready in about 30 days. Spray at 3% (300 ml per 10 L) at 15-day intervals; filter before spraying.

**Vermicompost:** partially decomposed farm waste + cow dung in shaded pits/beds, Eudrilus earthworms, keep moist; ready in ~60 days. 1–2 t/acre.

**Farmyard manure:** well-decomposed only (fresh dung brings weeds and termites). 5–10 t/acre before ploughing.

**Bio-fertilisers** (cheap from agriculture depots): Azospirillum / Rhizobium (by crop), phosphobacteria, and potash-mobilising bacteria — seed treatment or soil application mixed with FYM.

**Support:** Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) supports organic farmer clusters, including PGS-India certification. Ask the Agriculture Extension Centre about organic / natural farming programmes in your block.

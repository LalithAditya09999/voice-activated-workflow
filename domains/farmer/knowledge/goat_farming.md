## Practice
- Breeds: Kanni Adu, Kodi Adu, Salem Black (local, hardy); Tellicherry and Boer crosses grow faster but need more care.
- Housing: raised slatted floor, dry and airy; 1–1.5 sq m per adult.
- Feed: browsing plus agathi, subabul, glyricidia, Co-4 grass; salt lick or mineral mixture.
- Health: PPR vaccine, enterotoxaemia vaccine, deworming every 3–4 months (by faecal test if possible), clean water.
- Sell by weight, not by eye; festival seasons pay more.

## Support
- Ask the veterinary dispensary and the Animal Husbandry Department about goat unit schemes and livestock insurance.

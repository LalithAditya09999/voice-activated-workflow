**Common rotations in Tamil Nadu**
- Wetland (delta): Kuruvai paddy → Thaladi paddy → rice-fallow black gram / green gram. Or Samba paddy → pulses → summer green manure.
- Garden land (well irrigated): Paddy → cotton / maize → pulses; or banana → sugarcane → green manure → vegetables.
- Dryland: Millet (cumbu / cholam) → pulses (horse gram, cowpea) → groundnut, with intercrops like groundnut + redgram (6:1 rows) or sorghum + cowpea.

**Green manure:** daincha (Sesbania), sunhemp, kolinji (Tephrosia), sesbania. Sow ~25 kg seed / acre, plough in at 45–50 days / flowering, 1–2 weeks before transplanting.

**Rules of thumb:** alternate cereals with legumes; alternate deep-rooted with shallow-rooted; avoid following a crop with another from the same family (e.g. tomato → brinjal → chilli) to avoid carrying over soil-borne disease; keep a border crop / bund crop (e.g. marigold, castor, cowpea) to attract natural enemies.

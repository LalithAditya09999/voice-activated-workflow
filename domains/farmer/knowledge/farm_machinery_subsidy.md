## Buying with subsidy
- Scheme: Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (central) plus state schemes, through the district Agricultural Engineering office. Subsidy share depends on the machine and the farmer category; confirm the current rate.
- Documents: Aadhaar, land records (chitta), bank passbook, photo, quotation from an empanelled dealer; caste certificate if applicable.
- Buy only from empanelled dealers listed by the department; the subsidy is released after verification.

## Renting
- Custom hiring centres run by the department, FPOs or entrepreneurs rent tractors, harvesters and other machines by the hour or acre. Tamil Nadu has offered online booking of departmental machinery (ask about the current app or portal).

## Watch out
- Don't pay any "agent" to get a subsidy approved; the department process has no such fee.

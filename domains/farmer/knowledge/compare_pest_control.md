## Common (conventional) practice
Identify the pest → check the economic threshold → spray a product recommended for that crop and pest at the label dose → rotate chemical groups to slow resistance → respect the waiting period before harvest.

## Organic / natural practice
Healthy soil and balanced nutrition; mixed crops, border and trap crops (marigold, castor, cowpea); pheromone, yellow sticky and light traps; NSKE 5%, neem oil 3%; herbal repellent (ஐந்திலைக் கரைசல் / mooligai poochi viratti); Trichogramma cards, Pseudomonas, Trichoderma; birds perches.

## Side effects & risks
| | Chemical pesticides | Organic methods |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Fast knock-down | Slow; preventive |
| Friendly insects | Killed (spiders, ladybirds, wasps) → secondary pest outbreaks | Mostly spared |
| Resistance | Develops with repeated use of the same group | Rare |
| Food & health | Residues if waiting period ignored; poisoning risk for sprayer; water contamination | Low; some botanicals still irritate skin/eyes |
| Cost | Rising; repeated sprays | Low cash cost; more labour and attention |

**Never:** spray banned or "mixed cocktail" products sold by shops, spray in wind or without a mask, or reuse pesticide containers.

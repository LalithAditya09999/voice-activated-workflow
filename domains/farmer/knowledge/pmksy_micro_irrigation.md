**What it is:** Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana – "Per Drop More Crop" component pays most of the cost of drip and sprinkler systems. States add a top-up to the central share.

**Tamil Nadu (verify current year's G.O.):** small & marginal farmers have received up to 100% subsidy; other farmers about 75%, subject to unit-cost ceilings per hectare. Usually up to 5 ha per beneficiary.

**Who can apply:** any farmer with land records in their name (or a long-term lease where allowed) and an assured water source (well, borewell, canal, farm pond). Farmers who got micro-irrigation on the same land generally can't re-apply for about 7 years.

**Steps:**
1. Register at the block Horticulture / Agriculture office or on the TN Horticulture micro-irrigation (MIMIS) portal.
2. Choose a registered micro-irrigation company; they survey the field and prepare the design & quotation.
3. Officer inspects and approves → installation → joint verification → subsidy released to the company/farmer.

**Documents:** Chitta/Adangal, Aadhaar, bank passbook, photo, small/marginal farmer certificate (if claiming the higher rate), soil & water test report, FMB sketch.

**Tips:** Drip suits coconut, banana, sugarcane, vegetables, turmeric. Saves 30–50% water and labour on weeding. Don't pay "agents" — the office and the company handle the paperwork.

| Season (pattam) | Sowing–harvest | Typical crops / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kuruvai | Jun – Sep | Short-duration paddy (≈105–120 days). In the Cauvery delta it depends on Mettur dam water release (traditionally 12 June). |
| Samba | Aug – Jan | Main paddy season; long-duration varieties (≈130–160 days), including traditional varieties like Mappillai Samba, Seeraga Samba. Relies on north-east monsoon (Oct–Dec). |
| Thaladi | Sep/Oct – Feb | Medium-duration paddy transplanted right after Kuruvai harvest. |
| Navarai | Dec/Jan – Apr | Irrigated summer paddy, mainly in well-irrigated areas. |
| Aadi pattam | Jul – Aug sowing | Rainfed millets (ragi, cumbu/pearl millet, cholam/sorghum, samai, varagu), groundnut, pulses, cotton, vegetables. "ஆடிப் பட்டம் தேடி விதை." |
| Purattasi / Karthigai pattam | Sep – Nov sowing | NE-monsoon rainfed crops: millets, pulses, maize, groundnut, cotton in southern districts. |
| Thai / rice fallow | Jan – Feb | Black gram / green gram broadcast into standing or just-harvested paddy using residual moisture (delta practice). |

**Choosing:** match crop duration to assured water. Check the weekly TNAU agro-advisory and district varietal recommendations; ask the Agriculture Extension Centre for certified seed.

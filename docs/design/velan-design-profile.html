01 · FOUNDATIONS

Paper and ink,
a pinch of turmeric.

Calm warm paper, deep forest ink, sage for quiet words. Turmeric is the one accent and appears in small doses: the mic rim, the reply thread, the current step. Kumkum red is reserved for deadlines and errors; green for live and done.

02 · TYPE

Two voices in
every headline.

Public Sans for the interface, Hind Madurai for Tamil, Spectral for the few moments that deserve a printed feel (reference numbers, quotes). Headlines are weight 500 with −0.025em tracking: the first line in ink, the second in sage.

03 · LAYERS

Kolam lines are
our contour lines.

Rich surfaces are built in the same four layers, back to front. The art is always pulli kolam: dots and the loops drawn around them, faint enough to feel like texture, never like decoration competing with words.

1 · paper
2 · kolam lines + grain
3 · scrim behind words
4 · glass + content
  • Paper: one flat warm tone per section; alternate paper and sand bands.
  • Kolam + grain: line art at 10–13% ink, a fractal-noise grain at 18% multiply (8% screen at night).
  • Scrim: a soft paper-coloured ellipse behind every headline and a fade into the next section’s colour, so text stays readable and sections meet in seams, not edges.
  • Glass: the nav and floating cards blur what is behind them (backdrop-filter: blur(12px), 82% paper).
  • Shadows: long and soft, offset below the card: 0 30px 60px -40px in ink. Never hard drop shadows.

05 · MOTION

Arrive once,
breathe slowly.

Entrances play once with a long ease-out (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)): nav, headline, line, card, 150 ms apart. Ambient motion is slow enough to read as life. Only transform and opacity move. Reduced motion stops all of it.

House rules
for every Velan screen.

Tamil firstTamil leads, English follows smaller. Write how people speak, not how forms read.
One accentTurmeric in small doses. Red only for deadlines and errors; green only for live and done.
Two-tone headlinesInk line, then sage line. Weight 500, tight tracking, never all caps.
Kolam is textureFaint line art under a scrim. Never behind body text without one.
The turn is the layoutLatest reply largest, results above it, history tucked away, mic always in reach.
Calm motionEntrances once, ambient slow, transform and opacity only, reduced motion honoured.
Readable outdoors4.5:1 contrast for text, 56 px touch targets, 20 px+ for anything spoken.
Borrow, don’t copyTechniques from trycontour.com and the layered guide; none of their logos, art or words.

Inspired by the calm paper-and-ink editorial style of trycontour.com and the layered web style guide (art, grain, scrim, glass, seams, slow motion). Rebuilt for Velan with kolam line art and a Tamil Nadu palette.

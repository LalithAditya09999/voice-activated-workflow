# Voiceover and edit guide for the generated demo (2:44)

The story is **a farmer's ordinary day**, with Velan as an everyday helper. Crop insurance is
just one of the services on the home screen; it's deliberately not the focus.

The generated video (`velan-demo-everyday.mp4`) has captions and no sound.

## 1. Add one real-voice clip (about 8–10 seconds)
Record your laptop screen while you tap the mic and say **"சொட்டு நீர் பாசனம் வேண்டும்"** ("I want
drip irrigation") to the real app, and Velan replies. In the editor, put your clip **at 1:05** and
delete **1:05–1:13** of the generated video, the simulated version of the same moment. The total
stays under 3:00.

## 3. Your own footage (optional, for the first 15 seconds)
Everyday shots, not disasters. Get permission before filming anyone, and keep ID cards, forms
and phone numbers unreadable.
- **Morning:** a farmer walking the field (coconut, banana, grapes, paddy), drip lines, a
  borewell motor starting.
- **Errands:** at the agri-input shop buying seeds or fertiliser; a queue outside an e-Sevai
  centre (filmed from outside).
- **Paperwork:** hands holding an English form; an elderly parent asking a younger person to read
  something.
- **The change:** someone speaking Tamil into a phone, then nodding.
- **Life:** cattle, goats or chickens; produce being weighed at the market.

Hold the phone sideways, use 1080p, film in daylight and keep each clip to 3–6 seconds.

## 4. Export and upload
Export at 1080p, check it's **3:00 or less**, and upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**.
